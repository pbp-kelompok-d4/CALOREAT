Why use Caloreat?

The Ideal Portion

Healthy eating focuses on macro & micronutrients in every meal:

  • ½ Plate: Fruits & vegetables rich in vitamins, minerals, & fiber.
  • ¼ Plate: Complex carbs (brown rice, potatoes, oats) for sustained energy.
  • ¼ Plate: Lean protein (chicken, fish, eggs, tofu, tempeh) & repair.

Balancing your meal keeps blood sugar stable and prevents calorie surplus.

Know Your Calories Needs (BMR & TDEE)

Every body is unique. Knowing your calorie needs based on age, activity, and goals helps you eat mindfully without crash diets.

Good Nutrition for You & the Planet

Managing your portions is not just about weight, it also prevents food waste and reduces your daily carbon footprint. Good health, sustainable lifestyle!
